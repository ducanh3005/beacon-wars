📖 Beacon Wars — Tactical Field Manual

1-Paragraph Summary: 2–4 Commanders battle across a 37-hex arena to be the first to reach 25 Victory Points (VP) (or hold the highest VP at the end of Round 12). Each turn grants 3 Action Points (AP) and 1 + 2×Wells Energy (capped at 10⚡). Spend AP and Energy to Deploy units, launch deterministic flanking Strikes, raise protective Shields, or overcharge held Beacons with Pulse for burst VP and shockwave knockback. At the end of your turn, harvest +2 VP for controlling the Central Nexus (0,0) and +1 VP for each Outer Beacon.

1. DEPLOY (1 AP • 1⚡)

Add +1 Unit to a hex you control (max 4 units/hex) OR claim an adjacent neutral hex with 1 Unit. (If wiped to 0 hexes, perform an Orbital Drop onto any empty outer-ring hex.)

2. STRIKE (2 AP • 1⚡)

Attack an adjacent enemy hex from a friendly hex with 2+ Units. Zero RNG — pure geometry:
Attack = Attacker Units + Extra Friendly Hexes Adjacent to Target
Defense = Defender Units + (2 if Shielded)
• Attack > Defense: Capture hex with Units - 1 (1 stays behind).
• Tied: Both lose 1 unit.
• Lower: Attacker loses 1 unit.

3. SHIELD (1 AP • 2⚡)

Fortify one controlled hex with a +2 Defense Forcefield that lasts until the start of your next turn — ideal for holding contested Beacons against enemy Strikes.

4. PULSE (2 AP • 4⚡)

Overcharge all Beacons you hold for an immediate +3 VP per Beacon AND push adjacent unshielded enemy 1-unit scouts 1 hex away! Requires 1 turn to recharge after firing.
